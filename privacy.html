Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

In short: Dough Math collects none of your data. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics or other outside code. What you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Dough Math stores what you enter: your pantry items and what you paid for them, your recipes, your hourly rate, overhead and target margin, your business name and contact, and your orders with each customer's name and contact. All of it is stored only on your iPhone and in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer, if you use one). Dough Math does not sync it and never sends it to us.

You can delete any pantry item, recipe or order in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept.

Your customers' details

The names and contact details you enter for your customers are yours to look after. We never receive them and cannot see them.

Quotes and costing sheets

A PDF leaves Dough Math only when you tap Share and pick where it goes. Dough Math sends nothing by itself. The file is removed from the app once the share sheet closes.

The camera and receipts

If you scan a receipt, Dough Math uses the camera to read its lines and prices, on your iPhone. The photo is not saved, not added to your photo library and not sent anywhere. Once you close the review, the photo and its text are gone. For each line you match to a pantry item, Dough Math keeps the name the shop printed, so it can match it next time. Dough Math doesn't use your photo library or your microphone.

Pasting prices and ingredients

Dough Math reads your clipboard only when you tap to paste, and uses what it reads only to fill in the screen you are on.

Diagnostics

Dough Math keeps a short list of errors on your iPhone: what failed, the date and the app version. It holds no customer, order or recipe content. It goes nowhere unless you open Settings, tap Send diagnostics and share it yourself.

No account, no servers

Dough Math has no account to sign in to and no servers of its own. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Purchases

Dough Math Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account information. Apple gives developers anonymous sales totals.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage totals. They don't identify you, and we receive them only through Apple. You control this in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

How long we keep things

  • Everything on your iPhone is kept until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Who else is involved

Apple handles payments and, only if you opt in, crash reports and usage totals, under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Children

Dough Math is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email eighthdayapps@outlook.com. If you email us, we use your address only to reply to you.
