Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email eighthdayapps@outlook.com.

We reply within one business day. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Dough Math version (Settings, under About) and what you were doing when the problem happened. Never send card details or passwords.

If something failed to save, open Settings, tap Send diagnostics and share the log with us. It lists errors only, with no customer, order or recipe content.

Why does a recipe line show no cost?

Dough Math needs a price for the pantry item, and a way to get from the unit you bake in to the unit you buy in. If you buy flour by the pound and bake in cups, open the pantry item and give it grams a cup. Dough Math suggests a figure for common ingredients, and never guesses one it doesn't know.

A price has changed

Open the item in Pantry and tap Update price. Every recipe that uses it is costed again, and so is every order and shopping list.

How is the suggested price worked out?

Dough Math adds up the ingredients, your labor at the hourly rate in Settings and your overhead, then sets a price that leaves your target margin. Prices round up. You can give a recipe its own margin.

A receipt didn't read well

Lay the receipt flat in good light and fill the frame with it. A crumpled or faded receipt may miss lines. You check every line before any price changes, and you can fix a line or skip it. You can also paste prices from a spreadsheet, or type them.

Sending a quote

Open the order and tap Preview quote to see the PDF, then Share quote to send it by Messages, Mail or any app you pick. A quote shows what the customer pays. It never shows your costs or margins.

Dough Math Pro and refunds

Cancel: in Dough Math, open Settings and tap Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Not showing as Pro: open Settings, tap See Pro plans, then Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Refunds: purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose "Request a refund".

If Pro ends, nothing you made is deleted or locked. Free stops you adding more than 3 recipes and 3 active orders.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your pantry, recipes and orders are in your iPhone backup. Restore the new iPhone from it and they come with you. Dough Math has no account and no sync of its own.
