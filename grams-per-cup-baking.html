What one level US cup of each ingredient weighs. These are the figures Dough Math suggests when you add a pantry item, rounded from common US baking weight charts.

Your own cup may weigh a little more or less, depending on how you fill it. A US cup is 236.6 ml and a tablespoon is a sixteenth of it. Ounces here are ounces of weight (28.35 g), not fluid ounces.

Weigh your own cup

  1. Stir the flour in its bag to loosen it.
  2. Spoon it into the cup until it heaps, then level it with a straight edge.
  3. Weigh it, tip it back, and do it twice more.
  4. Use the middle figure.

Scooping the cup straight through the bag packs the flour down, and can add 20 g or more a cup. Whichever way you fill it, fill it the same way every time.

Packed and pressed

Brown sugar is measured packed, pressed into the cup until it holds its shape, which is why it weighs more than granulated sugar. Butter is the easy one: a US stick is half a cup, 113 g.

To use these figures, read cups to grams for baking. To turn them into a cost, read what one recipe costs.
