Know what every bake costs.

Dough Math is an iPhone app for home bakers who sell what they bake. Enter what you pay for flour, butter and eggs, the way you buy them. Write your recipes in cups or grams. Dough Math works out what a batch costs and what to charge for a loaf.

When the price of butter goes up, change it once and every recipe that uses butter is costed again.

Add your orders and Dough Math writes the week's shopping list and a PDF quote you can send to your customer.

Get Dough Math on the App Store

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your prices, recipes and customers stay on your iPhone.
